Calculation

V2: free funds = totalAssets − remaining locked profit. Holder value = external shares × free funds / totalSupply × asset USD price. Legacy getter spellings are normalized; version 0.3.0 has no profit locking. V3: holder value follows shrinking effective share supply as shown below.

H = effective totalSupply − effective balanceOf(vault)
holderValue(t) = totalAssets × H / effectiveSupply(t) × assetUsdPrice
effectiveSupply(t) = snapshotSupply − additionalSharesUnlocked(t)

Both versioned V3 allocator and Tokenized Strategy self-balance getters already subtract unlocked shares. Subtracting unlockedShares again would overstate holder value. We preserve integer share accounting and contract rounding before USD conversion.

Which layer should supply the unlock rate?

Use the layer whose share value changes for external holders. The bottom strategy is not always that layer: a multi-strategy vault carries recorded debt and recognizes the strategy’s increased value when it processes a report. Either layer can have a zero unlock period.

Overlap example: Morpho Yearn OG USDC

Of its non-self-held shares, 95.0036% belongs directly to yvUSD and 3.5761% to USDC-2 at the pilot block. Only 1.4203% remains after those two known internal holders. Its $0.016008/s whole-holder unlock rate therefore leaves approximately $0.000227/s for the remaining holders, before checking any other internal positions. The parent vaults’ own schedules remain relevant: child appreciation is report-gated.

This is a partial attribution example, not a completed aggregate deduction. A $10k cutoff selects displayed roots; it must not exclude smaller intermediary strategies or internal holders needed to trace their exposure.

Reuse metrics ownership, align the baseline

The metrics TVL ledger already resolves direct and strategy-routed ownership, with shared-holder budgets and parent/child caps. Reuse its relationship graph and scope. However, its TVL starts from totalAssets, including locked profits; simply adding their future unlocking to that total would count them twice. For the chosen depositor-value counter, derive the baseline from non-self share-conversion values with the same ownership policy.

For wrappers whose underlying asset is another vault share, its USD conversion must follow the underlying share value recursively. Freezing that share-token price would miss this propagation. For ordinary same-asset strategies, child appreciation remains unreported until the parent processes it.

Validation and next steps

  • 120 Ethereum roots selected at one finalized block: 25 original V3 plus 95 V2, including factory and funded retired vaults. All selected share models match contract observations.
  • 100 V3 conversion comparisons and 380 V2 pricePerShare comparisons matched the models at snapshot and future timestamps against the same stored state.
  • 13 selected roots have no USD price in the captured responses. Price observations have different timestamps; see the raw snapshot before treating them as live market prices.
  • 42,240 holder/child pairs were checked with no unavailable reads; 40 positive balances were found and 30 positions received nonzero internal attribution. Strategy discovery and cross-chain ownership remain incomplete.
  • The next step is a consistent ownership graph plus the remaining chains, then a refreshable shared snapshot. This research does not replace the landing hero.
