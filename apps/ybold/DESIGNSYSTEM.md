# yBOLD lander design system

The page uses a flat, geometric DeFi aesthetic with large modular sections, strong color blocking, restrained rounded
elements, and generous whitespace. It intentionally avoids gradients, shadows, glass effects, and generic SaaS card
layouts.

## Color system

```text
--background: #f2efe9
--surface: #ffffff
--navy: #111b4d
--yearn-blue: #0657f9
--electric-blue: #405be5
--bold-green: #5fd67c
--yellow: #ffdc37
--muted-text: #858cae
--border: rgba(17, 27, 77, 0.12)
```

Yearn blue provides the primary identity. The cream background, navy typography, green accents, and geometric shapes
provide visual continuity for Liquity and BOLD users.

## Layout and interaction

The hero is an asymmetric two-column layout above 900px and becomes a single column on smaller screens, with the
product story before the vault widget. Buttons are pill-shaped, cards use modest radii, and metrics use aligned rows
and thin dividers. Keep touch targets large and horizontal padding at or above 20px.
